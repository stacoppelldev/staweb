The Society of St. Vincent de Paul, founded in 1833 by Blessed Frederic Ozanam, is a global Catholic organization comprising men and women of all ages. Dedicated to aiding the less fortunate, it operates in 150 countries with approximately one million volunteers. Named after its patron saint, St. Vincent de Paul, the organization collaborates with compassionate individuals worldwide, providing assistance to those in need without discrimination, recognizing the face of Jesus in every person.
